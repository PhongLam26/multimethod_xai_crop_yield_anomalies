# Reviewer Q&A: V3 Method Route

## Does the V1 crop result claim weather has no effect?

No. It says this state-level panel and locked protocol do not permit a
weather-specific observed-event explanation because Gate A and primary Gate B1
fail.

## Why is the synthetic benchmark needed?

It supplies known drivers and known invalid regimes, allowing false permission
and abstention behavior to be measured rather than inferred from a real panel.

## Why is the EIA result relevant?

It is an external forecasting domain where the same gated feature-group logic
passes on a locked temporal holdout. It tests the method, not agricultural
transfer or causal attribution.

## When is an explanation shown?

Only after the pre-specified predictive and feature-group gate conditions pass.
Otherwise the workflow emits ABSTAIN and retains any attribution only as a model
diagnostic.

## What remains unavailable?

County-level agricultural V2 evidence is blocked until official USDA NASS data
can be acquired through a valid API credential or a disk-feasible official bulk
download followed by coverage auditing.
