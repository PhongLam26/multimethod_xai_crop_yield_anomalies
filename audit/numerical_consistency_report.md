# Numerical Consistency Report

Generated evidence for the revised protocol is stored in `artifacts/`.

- `artifacts/data/detrending_audit.csv` and `audit/leakage_audit.csv` record one train-only trend and residual scale per crop--state series and fold.
- `artifacts/models/model_selection_log.csv` contains the pre-final-test model comparison for 2012--2015.
- `artifacts/metrics/same_task_baselines.csv` contains final-test baseline comparisons; `artifacts/predictions/final_test_row_level_predictions.csv` is the row-level source.
- `artifacts/metrics/tail_metrics.csv` is the authoritative source for lower-tail performance; it shows that no event-level weather explanation claim is supported by the locked residual forecast.
- `artifacts/metrics/bootstrap_ci.csv` uses year-block resampling, not row-wise resampling.

The existing `outputs/xai/` figures and tables were generated by the retrospective pipeline and must not be cited as final scientific evidence until P1-3 through P2-16 are rerun under the revised target/split protocol. They are intentionally not treated as consistent with the revised manuscript claims.
