2. Định vị contribution mới của bản A
Không trình bày novelty như “train-only preprocessing” hoặc “locked testing”. Đây là safeguards. Contribution phải nằm ở policy về phạm vi claim và cách policy được đánh giá.
2.1. Bốn contribution nên giữ
Một claim-eligibility protocol ở cấp nghiên cứu cho predictive XAI. Protocol phân biệt mô tả fitted function, overall predictive adequacy và weather-specific incremental predictive reliance.
Một Gate B1 đúng estimand: so sánh Full với Metadata-only để đo giá trị dự báo tăng thêm của weather sau context; Weather-only với Metadata-only chỉ là representation diagnostic.
Một calibration suite gồm simulation với cơ chế sinh dữ liệu đã biết, ablation của các policy, agricultural abstention cases và một positive sanity-check permission case.
Một audit trail liên kết split, target construction, model selection, paired predictions, bootstrap draws và manuscript claims; đây là cơ chế truy vết, không phải bằng chứng khoa học tự thân.
2.2. Contribution paragraph đề xuất
This paper makes four contributions. First, we formulate a study-level claim-eligibility protocol for post-hoc explanations of predictive models. The protocol distinguishes descriptions of a fitted function from claims about locked-period outcomes. Second, we separate overall predictive adequacy (Gate A) from the conditional incremental value of weather beyond metadata (Gate B1); a weather-only comparison is retained only as a representation diagnostic. Third, we evaluate the decision rule in simulations with known data-generating mechanisms and in three empirical settings that include both abstention and permission outcomes. Fourth, we release an audit trail that binds each reported gate, interval, prediction vector, and target definition to a reproducible artifact. Train-only preprocessing and locked testing are safeguards that support these contributions rather than standalone claims of novelty.
Codex không được chép máy móc nếu cấu trúc cuối thay đổi. Giữ ý và viết sao cho phù hợp số trang. Trong abstract và conclusion, không cần lặp đủ bốn contribution.

3.2. Giải thích Metadata-only trùng zero baseline
Không gọi đây đơn giản là “model collapse” nếu chưa kiểm tra prediction vector. Trước hết phải xác nhận:
Maximum absolute difference giữa Metadata-only predictions và zero-residual vector.

Residual training mean theo crop-state có đúng xấp xỉ 0 do OLS trend có intercept hay không.
Suggested manuscript wording after numerical verification:Because the target is an OLS residual constructed separately within each crop–state series, its training mean is approximately zero within each fitted series. The metadata variables are time-invariant identifiers and provide no within-series temporal signal. Under the locked ExtraTrees configuration, the metadata-only predictions therefore reduce to the zero-residual vector on the final rows (maximum absolute difference: [INSERT AUDITED VALUE]). Consequently, Gate B2 is numerically identical to Gate A in this panel, although the two contrasts answer different conceptual questions. Gate B1 remains distinct because the Full model can use weather jointly with metadata.

Báo cáo số row theo history 3–4, 5–7, ≥8.
Báo cáo event membership changes khi đổi min history.

Suggested wording:The favorable interval under the eight-year history rule does not overturn the primary decision because it evaluates a different locked population. It nevertheless suggests a design hypothesis: longer series may stabilize both detrending and residual-scale estimation. A future prospective study should prespecify a minimum-history requirement rather than treat it as a post-hoc filter.
4.7. Nêu rõ raw-target và standardized-target đổi dấu
The standardized-target sensitivity changes the point-estimate direction: the raw-residual model is slightly better than zero at the point estimate (ΔRMSE = −0.005), whereas the model fit directly to training-standardized residuals is slightly worse (ΔRMSE = +0.030). This reversal shows that target scale and series-specific variance estimates materially affect the predictive question; neither interval supports a primary pass.
4.8. Small-number-of-blocks limitation
Nói thẳng primary temporal bootstrap chỉ có 10 calendar-year blocks.
Giải thích tại sao year-block vẫn là primary: giữ cross-sectional dependence trong cùng năm.
Nhấn state-year và crop-state resampling là sensitivity, không tương đương hoàn toàn với temporal block.
Cân nhắc leave-one-year-out influence plot hoặc exact/randomization-based check.
Thêm nguồn primary về cluster/bootstrap inference với số cluster nhỏ nếu phù hợp; không trích blog.
Suggested limitation sentence:The primary interval resamples only ten calendar-year blocks. This preserves cross-sectional dependence within a year but limits the precision of tail inference. State–year and crop–state resampling produce the same qualitative decision, although they encode different dependence assumptions and do not remove the small-number-of-years limitation.
4.9. Kể đúng rolling-origin result
Temporal performance is directionally unstable. Three folds favor the model at the point-estimate level, but only the 2013–2015 fold has an interval below zero. In contrast, the 2007–2009 fold significantly favors the zero-residual baseline (ΔRMSE = 0.017, 95% CI [0.003, 0.028]).

7. Khung Abstract mới
Codex phải viết lại abstract sau khi mọi số đã được sinh lại. Dưới đây là khung nội dung, không phải bản cuối cố định.
Post-hoc explanations of a predictive model should not be used as evidence about observed yield losses when the model lacks predictive adequacy for the same locked target. We formulate a study-level claim-eligibility protocol that separates overall predictive adequacy (Gate A) from the incremental value of weather beyond metadata (Gate B1). The primary audit uses 1,257 U.S. crop–state–year observations for four crops from 1990–2025, with train-only detrending, validation-based selection, and a locked 2016–2025 evaluation. The selected weather-only ExtraTrees model does not outperform the zero-residual baseline (ΔRMSE = −0.005, 95% CI [−0.019, 0.009]), and the Full model does not establish incremental weather value over metadata (ΔRMSE = −0.012, 95% CI [−0.029, 0.002]). Agricultural explanations are therefore reported as descriptions of the fitted function rather than evidence about observed events. Simulations with known data-generating mechanisms evaluate false permission and false abstention across prespecified regimes, while county-level agriculture and a PJM demand sanity check illustrate abstention and permission outcomes. The protocol supports scoped predictive-reliance claims; it does not convert feature attribution into causal evidence.
