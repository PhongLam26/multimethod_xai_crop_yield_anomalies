3. Định vị đóng góp — việc quan trọng nhất
Rủi ro lớn nhất không phải số liệu sai, mà là reviewer đọc specificity 28.7% rồi kết luận: “gate không thật sự chặn”. Codex phải viết lại câu chuyện theo logic sau:
Ungated explanations falsely permit 240/240 structurally invalid runs.
Observable A+B+E gating reduces false permission to 171/240, tức loại được 69/240 invalid runs.
Phần invalid còn lại tập trung ở leakage, omitted confounding, correlated features và shift — các lỗi có thể giữ nguyên hoặc làm tăng predictive accuracy.
Vì vậy predictive gating là điều kiện cần cho claim discipline, nhưng không thể là structural-validity detector.
Design-stage checks về leakage, confounding, measurement validity và distribution shift vẫn bắt buộc.
CÂU CHỐT PHẢI XUẤT HIỆN SỚM
Predictive gating reduces over-permission relative to ungated explanation, but it cannot detect structural violations that preserve or improve predictive performance. It is therefore necessary for claim discipline, not sufficient for scientific validity.
3.1 Abstract — mục tiêu và bản nháp gợi ý
Rút Abstract còn khoảng 190–205 từ. Mở đầu bằng phát hiện và contribution, không mở đầu bằng danh sách quy trình. Phải có 240/240 → 171/240, negative agricultural result và giới hạn necessary-but-not-sufficient.
Draft target (Codex may tighten for page fit, but preserve the logic and numbers):Post-hoc explanations can appear coherent even when the fitted model lacks locked-period evidence for the outcome claim being made. We propose a reproducible claim-eligibility audit that separates overall predictive adequacy (Module A), incremental feature-group value (Module B), event recovery (Module E), and descriptive diagnostics (Module D). In synthetic stress tests, ungated explanations falsely permit 240/240 structurally invalid runs, whereas the observable A+B+E policy reduces false permission to 171/240 while retaining 88.9% sensitivity. The remaining 71.2% false-permission rate and 28.7% specificity show a principled boundary: predictive gates cannot reliably detect leakage, omitted confounding, or distributional violations that preserve or improve predictive performance. We apply the audit to a leakage-safe U.S. crop-state-year panel. A validation-selected Weather-only ExtraTrees model fails locked overall adequacy, Full-minus-Metadata incremental weather value also fails, and the complete below-trend event-recovery rule does not pass. Consequently, post-hoc explanations are retained only as fitted-function diagnostics. The contribution is a locked, same-row, paired-uncertainty protocol that reduces unsupported interpretation while making explicit which validity checks must remain at the study-design stage.
3.2 Mở đầu Discussion — đoạn bắt buộc
The synthetic benchmark clarifies the scope of the proposed gate. Relative to ungated explanation, the observable A+B+E policy reduces false permission from 240/240 to 171/240 invalid runs. The remaining false permissions are concentrated in regimes such as leakage, omitted confounding, correlated features, and geographic or temporal shift, which may preserve or even improve predictive performance. Predictive gating should therefore be interpreted as a necessary claim-discipline layer, not as a detector of structural validity; design-stage leakage, confounding, measurement, and distribution-shift checks remain independently required.
3.3 Conclusion — đoạn bắt buộc
The agricultural audit supports model description only: Modules A, B, and E fail on the locked state panel, and the separately locked county check remains inconclusive under Module A. More generally, the gate reduces unsupported permission relative to ungated explanation but does not establish structural validity. The synthetic reduction from 240/240 to 171/240 false permissions demonstrates practical claim discipline, while the remaining failures show why leakage, omitted confounding, measurement validity, and distribution-shift checks must be enforced at the design stage.

5. Câu chữ chính xác cần chèn hoặc làm rõ
5.1 Module B interval
The Module B interval is computed directly from paired Full-minus-Metadata predictions on the same locked rows. It is numerically identical to Full-minus-zero in this panel because the Metadata-only prediction vector coincides with the zero-residual baseline to floating-point precision (maximum absolute difference approximately 10^-13).
5.2 Lý do giữ Module D
Module D is retained as a general representation diagnostic for settings in which metadata carries predictive information. It coincides numerically with Module A only in this panel because Metadata-only predictions collapse to the zero-residual baseline.
5.3 ROC/PR không mâu thuẫn Module E
Weakly positive ROC-AUC and PR-AUC summarize global discrimination across all locked rows, whereas Module E requires paired error improvement and recovery of the pre-specified tail beyond rank and top-k null checks. The two results therefore address different questions and are not contradictory.
5.4 Bảo vệ Module E
The components of Module E target distinct failure modes: tail RMSE/MAE test error magnitude, rank statistics test ordering, and chance-adjusted top-k checks test prioritization of the most important events. The module is not mechanically impossible to pass: in the synthetic benchmark, moderate-signal, strong-signal, and valid imbalanced-tail regimes pass Module E in 30/30 seeds.
5.5 Bootstrap và power
With only ten locked calendar-year blocks, interval precision and statistical power are necessarily limited. We nevertheless retain year-level paired resampling because it preserves the cross-sectional dependence shared by crop-state rows within the same calendar year.
5.6 Bảo vệ trước “setup được dựng để fail”
The negative agricultural result does not imply that weather lacks scientific relevance or that a stronger model cannot exist. The audit asks a narrower and model-specific question: whether the validation-selected fitted model has sufficient locked evidence to support the requested claim. The separately locked county check shows that finer resolution does not automatically rescue overall adequacy, while the PJM permission case demonstrates that the policy does not always abstain when signal is strong.
5.7 Fig. 2 base value caption
The displayed grouped SHAP contributions sum to 0.217; adding the SHAP base value of approximately -0.008 yields the reported prediction of 0.209. The observed residual is -0.510.
