# Limitations

- The primary locked-test Gate A fails, so fitted-model explanations are not
  evidence of observed-event drivers.
- Primary Gate B1 fails; Gate B2 is sensitivity-only and cannot rescue B1.
- State-level coordinates and aggregated weather can miss within-state exposure
  heterogeneity.
- Results depend on the specified data vintage, train-only detrending protocol,
  crop-state histories, and fixed prospective split.
- Sensitivity analyses describe protocol robustness; they are not additional
  opportunities to claim primary success.
