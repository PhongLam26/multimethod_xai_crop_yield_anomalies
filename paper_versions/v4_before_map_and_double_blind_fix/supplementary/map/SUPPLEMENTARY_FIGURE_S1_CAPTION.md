# Supplementary Figure S1

Fig. S1. State-level heterogeneity on the locked 2016--2025 crop panel. Colors show the descriptive difference in RMSE between the validation-selected Weather-only model and the zero-residual baseline within each state; negative values favor the model. Labels report the state abbreviation and locked test row count. Gray states are outside the study panel. These subgroup estimates illustrate spatial heterogeneity and are not separate module decisions. Geometry provenance and hashes are recorded in the accompanying map manifest.
